Support for MiniMedia

Where do my albums come from?

MiniMedia shows the albums you create in Apple's Photos app, including iCloud Shared Albums. Make an album in Photos and it will appear in MiniMedia automatically. MiniMedia does not work with Google Photos or other services; save the pictures you want to your Photos library and add them to an album.

I forgot my passcode

Delete MiniMedia and reinstall it from the App Store. Your photos are not affected. To get MiniMedia+ back, open Settings in MiniMedia and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I keep my child inside MiniMedia?

Use iOS Guided Access. In MiniMedia, open Settings and tap Device Lock Setup for step-by-step instructions.
