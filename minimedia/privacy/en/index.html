Privacy Policy for MiniMedia

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

MiniMedia does not collect, share, or store any personal data. Your photos and videos are shown directly from your device's Photos library and never leave your device. No user data is transmitted, tracked, or linked to your identity.

Thank you for choosing MiniMedia! This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Don't Collect

MiniMedia does not collect, store, or share any personal information from its users. The app has no user accounts, does not connect to any server operated by us, and does not transmit any data over the internet.

2. Photos and Videos

MiniMedia requests access to your Photos library so it can display the albums you choose to share with your child. Your photos and videos are read and displayed only on your device. They are never uploaded, copied to a server, or shared with us or anyone else. You can change or remove MiniMedia's access to your photos at any time in your device's Settings.

3. Settings and Passcode

Your preferences (such as which albums are visible, your theme, playback options, and session timer) are stored only on your device. If you set a passcode, it is stored securely in your device's Keychain and never leaves your device. Face ID or Touch ID, if used, is handled entirely by iOS; MiniMedia never receives your biometric data.

4. Purchases

MiniMedia+ is an optional in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's handling of purchases is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Analytics, Advertising, and Cookies

MiniMedia does not use any analytics tools, advertising, cookies, or tracking technologies, and does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data.

6. Children's Privacy

MiniMedia is designed to be used by children under the supervision of a parent or caregiver. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. If you believe that we may have inadvertently received such information, please contact us immediately and we will promptly delete it.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app or applicable laws. Any updates will be posted here with a revised "Last Updated" date. If significant changes are made, we may display a notice within the app to inform users.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or MiniMedia's privacy practices, please contact us at tani.creek.software@icloud.com.

By using MiniMedia, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

Thank you for using MiniMedia!
